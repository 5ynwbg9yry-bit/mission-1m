How it works

Four parts, from finding a coin to selling it. The scanner and the watcher run all the time on the trading box. Buys wait for the hourly run.

1

Scanner

Always on. It checks the market every minute and only flags coins. It never buys.

→
2

Safety check

Rug check, holder concentration, signs of a bundled launch, a funding check, and a test sell that actually goes through.

3

Entry decision

The hourly run reviews flagged coins and buys only when at least two signals line up and every safety gate passes.

4

Watcher

Always on. It handles the sells: the hard stop, the 2x and 4x take-profits, the trailing stop, the 25-minute time stop, and an instant exit if the creator sells or liquidity is pulled.

The rules

The working rulebook, fitted to a small account. Safety checks do not bend.

What it trades

  • Solana coins launched or migrated in the last 6 hours, on pump.fun, Raydium, or Meteora
  • At least $25K of liquidity and $10K of trading in the last 5 minutes
  • At least 200 holders
  • The creator can't freeze tokens or mint more
  • Liquidity is burned or locked

Buy signals

  • Needs at least two of these together
  • 5-minute volume more than 3× the hourly average
  • More than 65% of trades are buys
  • Holders up about 10% in 15 minutes
  • The number of separate buyers is rising
  • Never chase a coin already up 300% in the past hour unless it pulls back first

Safety gates

  • A RugCheck pass
  • The creator holds under 5%
  • The top 10 holders hold under 35%
  • No bundled launch
  • A funding check of the big holders
  • A test sell quote that goes through
  • Fees stay around a cent per trade

Sizing

  • About 25% of the account per trade, so the bet grows as the account grows
  • At most 2 positions open at a time
  • After two losses in a row, cut the size in half until a win
  • If the account falls 30% below its high, pause and rethink
  • Profits roll into the next trade

Exits

  • Sell everything at −12%
  • Sell 40% at 2×, and 30% at 4×
  • Let the last 30% run with a stop that follows 25% below the price
  • Get out after 25 minutes if the trade isn't up at least 10%
  • Sell right away if the creator sells or the liquidity gets pulled

Not used: leveraged futures. A wipe on a small account isn't worth it.
